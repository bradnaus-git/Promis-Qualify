# Content Orchestrator Skill: Promis Qualify AS

## 1. Tone of Voice & Personality
- **Authoritative yet Approachable**: We don't use hype or jargon without substance. We speak with the quiet confidence of senior practitioners who have managed Norway's most demanding IT deliveries.
- **Norwegian Business Culture**: Emphasize transparency (*åpenhet*), work-life balance (*balanse*), inclusion (*inkludering*), and dedication (*engasjement*).
- **Public Sector Resonance**: Speak directly to public procurement leads (offentlige innkjøpere), program leaders, and digital transformation executives. Emphasize compliance with Digdir, risk reduction, ISTQB standards, and independent advice.
- **Private Sector Energy**: Emphasize speed to market, automated test pipelines, ROI on quality assurance, and preventing costly launch defects.

## 2. Terminology Glossary
- **Testledelse** (Test Management): Holistic oversight, test strategy, risk-based prioritization.
- **Testrådgivning** (Test Advisory): Maturity assessments, tool selection, QA transformation.
- **Testledelse i byggeprosjekter**: Commissioning, systematic completion, testing IT/IoT/BMS in hospitals, airports, and smart buildings.
- **Big Testing**: 360-degree risk assessment, trial operations (*prøvedrift*), and operational readiness for large-scale cyber-physical/hardware-software systems.
- **ISTQB Advanced**: Certification benchmark ensuring all consultants operate at the highest industry echelon.
